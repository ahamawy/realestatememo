Act I The Pattern
This has happened before. Twice.
Tech wealth cycles create concentrated liquidity events. That capital migrates, predictably, into scarce real assets. The destination changes. The pattern does not.
1997 – 2005

The Dotcom Boom

IPO wealth to San Francisco
2.2×
SF median home price increase
258,000 new millionaires created in the Bay Area at peak. Median home price surged from $290K to $640K. Wealth concentrated in a 50-mile radius around Sand Hill Road. Post-crash, prices never returned to pre-boom levels. The floor was permanently reset.
Details
2020 – 2025

The Crypto Boom

Token wealth to Dubai and Miami
3.3×
Dubai price/sqft increase from trough
Zero income and capital gains tax drew crypto wealth to the UAE. Over $110B in crypto capital flowed into Dubai real estate. Entire neighborhoods repriced. The globally mobile, tax-sensitive buyer chose Dubai over London and Singapore, reshaping the market permanently.
Details
2024 – 2032

The AI Boom

AI wealth to global scarcity assets
$10.7T
Projected AI-generated wealth across 5 hubs
This cycle is different in one critical way: AI wealth is being created simultaneously across five global hubs. The capital is more distributed, the buyers are younger and more lifestyle-driven, and they are choosing assets based on scarcity, tax efficiency, and quality of life, not proximity to an office.
Details
The pattern is clear: concentrated wealth creation leads to lifestyle migration, then price explosion in supply-constrained markets.

The question is not whether this happens. It is which assets capture the flow.
